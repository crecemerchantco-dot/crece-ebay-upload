Crece Merchant Co - eBay Uploader Privacy Policy

This private, single-seller application is used by its owner to upload product images to eBay.

OAuth credentials and tokens are processed on the server. User access and refresh tokens are held temporarily in server memory and are lost on restart or disconnect.

Product photos are transmitted to eBay and are not stored on our server. We do not collect buyer, order, or customer profile data or retain an eBay user database.

Uploaded photos and their resulting URLs are subject to eBay's policies. Our hosting provider may retain ordinary request logs.

For privacy questions, contact crecemerchantco@gmail.com.
